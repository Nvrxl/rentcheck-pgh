    How we score this

    Draft: final wording coming soon.

    The rating is driven mainly by building and fire safety violations (things like structural problems, fire hazards, electrical and heating issues), especially ones that are still unresolved.

    Most city records are about trash, weeds and debris outside the property. By our count that is about half of all records. They are still listed in the report, but they count much less, because they say little about whether an apartment is safe to live in.

    Everything is plain rules written in code. No AI or language model is involved, and the same records always give the same rating.

    A rating is a starting point for your own questions, not a verdict on the landlord. "No records found" is never treated as safe.

    Data: City of Pittsburgh via WPRDC. Not legal advice. A violation is not proof of a bad landlord.
